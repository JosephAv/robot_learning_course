Course Description

This course will provide foundational knowledge on Physical AI and high-level control, aimed at developing embodied agents able to perceive, learn, and act in the real world. Lectures focus on machine and deep learning solutions for autonomous systems, especially Reinforcement and Imitation Learning techniques behind today's generalist robot policies.

  • Physical AI
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • Imitation Learning
  • Sim-to-Real
  • Vision-Language-Action
  • World Models

Course Modules

From the fundamentals of robot sensing and control to cutting-edge policy learning and Physical AI foundation models.

  • Foundations of sensing and actuation
  • Kinematics and dynamics of autonomous systems
  • Motor control
  • Introduction to ROS programming (optional)
  • Gaussian Filters, Kalman Filter, Particle Filter (optional)

  • Supervised Learning for Robotics
  • Imitation Learning vs Reinforcement Learning
  • Markov Decision Processes

  • Planning by Dynamic Programming
  • Model-Free Prediction
  • Model-Free Control
  • Value Function Approximation

  • Finite Difference Policy Gradient
  • Monte-Carlo Policy Gradient
  • Actor-Critic approaches
  • Natural gradient
  • Deep policy networks

  • Model-based learning
  • Integrated Architectures
  • Learning and Planning
  • Real and simulated experience
  • Monte-Carlo Tree Search
  • Go use-case

  • Intro to bandits
  • Simple naïve exploration (𝜖-greedy)
  • Optimistic approaches
  • Probability matching & Information Value
  • Multi-armed Bandits
  • Contextual Bandits

  • Behavior cloning
  • Imitation Learning (DAgger)
  • Generative IL (Diffusion Policies)
  • Inverse RL (Max-Entropy IRL, AIRL)
  • GAIL (Generative Adversarial Imitation Learning)

  • Sim-to-Real Transfer
  • Vision and Graph-Based RL
  • World Models
  • Vision-Language-Action Models

  • Optimal Control
  • Reward Shaping
  • Q-Learning (Value-Based RL)
  • Reinforce, PPO, SAC (Policy Gradients)
  • Sim-to-Real Policy Transfer
